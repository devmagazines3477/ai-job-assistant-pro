Privacy policy

AI Job Assistant Pro, Chrome extension. Last updated: October 2026.

What the extension stores

Your profile (name, email, phone, location, skills, background), your resume details and photo, your saved jobs, your settings, your Gemini API key and your Pro license key are stored only in your browser using Chrome's extension storage. We do not run a server that receives this data, and we cannot see it.

What is sent to other services

When you click an AI button, the extension sends the question or text you selected, the job title, company and job description from the LinkedIn page, and the profile details needed for the answer to Google's Gemini API, using the API key you provided. Google processes this under its own terms and privacy policy. Nothing is sent to us.

When you use the Ask AI button in a post, comment or message box on LinkedIn, the extension sends the text currently in that box and, for comments, a short part of the nearby post text to Google's Gemini API, so it can suggest wording. This happens only when you click the button. The extension does not read your messages, posts or feed in the background.

The extension reads LinkedIn pages only to find text boxes and application form fields, to read job details, and to place its buttons. It does not collect your browsing history and does not send anything to us.

Payments

Pro payments are handled by PayPal outside the extension. The extension never sees your payment details. Pro license keys are checked locally inside the extension.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, use it for advertising, track you, or run analytics in the extension.

Your control

You can delete all stored data at any time by removing the extension or clearing its data in Chrome. You can delete your Gemini API key from Google AI Studio.
